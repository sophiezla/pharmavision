## 4. Anomalies, failed runs, open problems

1. **The development pool is at the ceiling.** Most pretrained arms reach pooled AUROC 0.994–1.000 and are statistically tied with the best (`swin-tiny/ft-tuned`, 0.9996). Only zero-shot CLIP (0.702), the small CNN, the handcrafted models and ResNet-50 are significantly worse. This is consistent with ADR-005: the classes are separable by acquisition (file format, size) in this pool.
2. **The external sets disagree with the development ranking.** Arms with high Mendeley specificity have low Split E recall, and vice versa. Examples:
    - the CLIP linear probe has Mendeley specificity 0.19 and Split E recall 0.78–0.91;
    - DINOv2 fine-tuning has 0.81–0.91 and 0.25–0.30.

    No arm reaches 0.5 on all four external columns; the best minimum is `efficientnetv2-s/ft` at 0.499. Per ADR-005, this does not drive any decision, but it is the central caveat for every later result.
3. **Tuning did not help.** The tuning objective (fold-0 val log-loss) saturated near 1e-5 for the fine-tuned backbones, so the chosen trials carry little information. The tuned settings are recorded and the defaults kept (ADR-016).
4. **E3 found no label issues.** cleanlab flagged 0 images; only the 2 `not_a_drug` images were on the review sheet, and both were removed.
5. **Operational:**
    - 30 `checkpoint_path.txt` files came back from the GPU PC with CRLF line endings. They were verified identical to the committed files and restored; the trainer now writes LF (bc1f46c).
    - The GPU benchmark crashed on the small CNN (a local, not a pretrained, backbone); it now benchmarks pretrained backbones only.
    - The laptop was low on memory throughout (free RAM often below 2 GB), so laptop CPU runs used small batches, one job at a time.

## 6. Proposed plan for Phase 3 (approved 2026-10-13)

- **E5 training recipe first**, on the global-only model (EfficientNetV2-S + LoRA r8, ADR-015): default plus 8 single-change arms. A component is kept only if it improves pooled AUROC per the protocol; improving components are combined and verified with one extra arm (`docs/experiments/E05.md`).

- **One GPU kit** runs the E3 retrain on data v2 → E5 → recipe decision → E4, all on data v2.
- **GPU-hour estimate:** from the E2 runs on the RTX 5060 (EfficientNetV2-S LoRA r8: about 285 s per run, 15 s per epoch):
    - E5: about 12–16 h. That is 8 single-change arms at about 1.2 h each, plus SupCon at about 3–4 h, plus the combination arm if one is needed.
    - E4 without the 896 px arm: about 35–45 h. Each arm costs about 5 backbone passes per image, so about 6 h per arm; the arm without the local branch is about 1.5 h.
    - The 896 px / 4×4 arm: about 17 passes per image, roughly 20–30 h on its own. It runs last.
    - Total: about 3–4 days. Fold-0 times are logged by the kit and will replace this estimate in the Phase 3 report.
