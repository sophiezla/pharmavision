## Intended use

Research on screening photographs of medicine packaging (boxes and blister packs) for signs of
counterfeiting, and a demonstration app that answers `Likely authentic`, `Likely counterfeit`
or `Uncertain — verify manually`. A positive or uncertain answer is a reason to ask a
pharmacist or the national medicines regulator, nothing more.

## Out-of-scope uses

- Deciding whether a medicine is safe to take, or any clinical or regulatory decision.
- Packaging, products, cameras or sources unlike the development data (see Known failure
  modes: performance drops sharply on other sources).
- Settings with an adaptive adversary who can query or inspect the model (E15).
- Pills or tablets outside their packaging; chemical authenticity.

## Data

Development pool and locked test set: Kaggle `kaggle_fake_real` only (ADR-001), deduplicated by
perceptual hash into groups (ADR-006); 15% of groups locked as the test set, the rest split into
5 grouped, stratified folds with train / val / cal inner splits (CLAUDE.md section 5). External
sets (never used for training, selection or calibration): Mendeley splits C and D (authentic)
and Split E (counterfeit; permission-restricted). Details and licences: `docs/DATA_CARD.md`.

## Known failure modes

**Conformal coverage on the locked test set.** Per-run coverage at alpha 0.10 is below the nominal 0.90 for final model (EfficientNetV2-S), E1 best (ConvNeXt-T, data v2), E16 student (MobileNetV3-L, KD T=4) (column `conformal coverage (per run, alpha 0.10)` in the locked-test table above), and lower than each model's out-of-fold coverage during development (`e10_conformal`, `e16_reliability`). The test set has 95 images, so the estimate is noisy; prediction sets should not be read as a guaranteed 90% coverage.

## Ethical considerations

A false `Likely authentic` on a counterfeit could give false reassurance; the app therefore
abstains through the conformal set, rejects unfamiliar inputs, and states its intended use. Split
E images (counterfeit alerts) are permission-restricted and never leave the owner's machines.
