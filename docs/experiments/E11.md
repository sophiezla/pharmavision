# E11 · OOD guard and the end-to-end system

Written before the experiment runs (PLAN 5.5); the Results section is added afterwards.

## Question

Which score best rejects images that are not medicine packaging photos from this distribution,
and what does the full chain (OOD guard -> model -> temperature -> conformal) change compared
with the bare classifier?

## Hypotheses

- Feature-space scores (Mahalanobis, kNN) separate OOD images better than output-based scores
  (MSP, energy), especially for near OOD.
- Near OOD (other packaged products) is harder than far OOD (textures, scenes) for every score.
- The full system has a much lower error rate on issued predictions than the bare classifier on
  a stream that contains OOD images.

## Data

- In-distribution (ID) test: each fold model's held-out fold. ID reference for thresholds: its
  `val` images. Feature statistics: its training images (evaluation transforms).
- OOD (`data/ood/`, PLAN 1.9, `results/tables/ood_sets.md`): near = 500 Grocery Store
  "Packages" images; far = 249 DTD textures + 250 Open Images scenes.
- OOD split (ADR-026): each OOD set is split once 50/50 (stratified by source and class, seed
  42) into `select` and `eval`. `select` is used only to choose the guard's score for the
  end-to-end system; `e11_end_to_end` uses only `eval`. `e11_ood` reports every OOD image.

## Scores (`evaluation/ood.py`; higher = more OOD)

- MSP: -max(p, 1 - p).
- Energy (T = 1): the binary logit z is written as two class logits (-z/2, z/2); score =
  energy = -log(e^(-z/2) + e^(z/2)). For a binary model this ranks images exactly like MSP; it
  is reported for completeness.
- Mahalanobis: class means of the fold model's penultimate embeddings on its training images,
  shared covariance with Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage; score = minimum squared distance to a class
  mean.
- kNN: L2-normalized penultimate embeddings; score = distance to the 50th nearest training
  embedding.
- Ensemble disagreement: per fold, standard deviation of the three seeds' probabilities.
- The handcrafted model has no penultimate embedding: MSP, energy and disagreement only
  (ADR-026).
- Guard threshold: the score that accepts 95% of the fold model's `val` images.

## End-to-end system (PLAN 5.7; final model)

Per fold model of the final model: stream = held-out fold + every OOD `eval` image.
- Guard: the per-model score (MSP, energy, Mahalanobis, kNN) with the highest mean OOD AUROC
  (near and far together) on the `select` half for the final model; ties go to kNN.
- Accepted images: temperature-scaled probability (E10), conformal set from the method chosen
  in E10 at alpha = 0.10 (0.05 reported too); a singleton is an issued prediction, any other
  set an abstention.
- Bare classifier: every image gets a prediction at the val-selected threshold on the raw
  probability (as in Phases 2-4).
- Any issued prediction on an OOD image counts as an error.

## Metrics

- `e11_ood`: AUROC (ID vs OOD) and FPR at 95% TPR (fraction of OOD images accepted when 95% of
  ID images are accepted), near and far, per model × score; mean ± sd over the 15 fold models
  (over the 5 folds for disagreement). Also the OOD acceptance at the val-based threshold.
- `e11_end_to_end`: rejection rate (ID, OOD), abstention rate, issued rate, error rate on
  issued predictions; full system vs bare classifier; mean ± sd over the 15 fold models.

## Findings

- Feature-space scores are not uniformly better. Mahalanobis gives the best near-OOD AUROC for
  ConvNeXt (0.993) and DINOv2 (0.966), but for the final model MSP is best on near OOD (0.979 vs
  Mahalanobis 0.963, kNN 0.926), and MSP wins the pre-registered guard choice on the `select`
  half (0.9761; energy ties, as expected for a binary model). The first hypothesis is not
  supported for the final model.
- Near OOD is harder than far OOD for kNN in every model with embeddings (e.g. final model
  0.926 vs 0.993) and for Mahalanobis in three of four (Swin: 0.985 for both); for MSP it is
  not consistent (final model 0.979 near vs 0.973 far).
- The handcrafted model cannot act as its own guard: MSP AUROC 0.787 (near) and 0.770 (far).
- End to end (alpha 0.10), the full system issues predictions on 0.199 of the stream with error
  0.184 on issued predictions, against 0.829 for the bare classifier, which issues on everything.
  The guard rejects 0.766 of OOD images and 0.052 of ID images. Almost every remaining error is
  an accepted OOD image (OOD among issued 0.183). The hypothesis of a much lower error on issued
  predictions holds, mainly through rejection.
- At alpha 0.05 the system issues more (0.300) and errs more (0.337) than at alpha 0.10: the
  abstention rate falls (0.058 vs 0.159), consistent with fewer empty sets at the larger
  quantile (E10, final model: empty-set rate 0.035 vs 0.083 on held-out images). Which stream
  images change from abstained to issued was not broken down.
