# E12 · Faithfulness of saliency maps

Written before the experiment runs (PLAN 6.1-6.4); the Results section is added afterwards.
Runs in the combined Phases 6-8 kit (ADR-027).

## Question

Do the heatmaps of the models actually reflect what drives their decisions, and do they point at
the edited region of a synthetic counterfeit?

## Hypotheses

- Every gradient- or attention-based method beats the random-map baseline on deletion and
  insertion AUC.
- Grad-CAM-type maps of the final model lose their similarity to the original maps as layers are
  randomized (they pass the sanity check); integrated gradients may stay partly image-driven.
- Localization on synthetic edits is weak for small text edits and better for large region edits.

## Models and methods

No training. The methods apply to different architectures, so each model gets the methods that
exist for it (`interpret/cams.py`, `interpret/attention_rollout.py`):

| Model (fold models) | Methods |
|---|---|
| Final model, `e05_efficientnetv2-s_default` (15) | Grad-CAM, Grad-CAM++, integrated gradients, random |
| DINOv2-S, `p4ref_dinov2-vits14_ft` (15) | attention rollout, integrated gradients, random |
| E4 multi-branch, `e04_multibranch_full` (15) | MIL tile attention, integrated gradients, random |

- Grad-CAM / Grad-CAM++ (`pytorch-grad-cam`) on the backbone's last feature map (`bn2`).
- Integrated gradients (Captum), 32 steps, baseline = the blurred image, channel-summed
  absolute attribution.
- Attention rollout (Abnar & Zuidema, 2020): 0.5 I + 0.5 head-mean attention per block,
  row-normalized, multiplied over the blocks; class-token row over the patches.
- MIL tile attention: the 2 x 2 tile weights painted over the tiles (448 px input).
- Random: 14 x 14 uniform noise upsampled bilinearly, seeded by image id.
- Maps explain the predicted class at logit 0 (counterfeit if logit >= 0, else authentic).

## Metrics (`evaluation/faithfulness.py`)

- **Deletion / insertion AUC** on every held-out image of every fold model: pixels ranked by
  the map; in 5% steps (21 points) the top pixels are replaced by the blurred image (Gaussian,
  sigma 5 px at model-input resolution) or copied into it; score = predicted-class probability;
  trapezoid AUC. Lower deletion and higher insertion = more faithful.
- **Model randomization** (Adebayo et al., 2018), fold 0 / seed 0 of each model, the first 50
  held-out images in image-id order: layers re-initialized cumulatively from the top (head,
  then the backbone's post-block layers, blocks last to first, stem); mean Spearman correlation
  of each map with the original after every step.
- **Localization** (PLAN 6.3) on the E14 synthetic counterfeits whose source image is in the fold
  model's held-out fold (never seen in training): pointing game (the map's maximum lies inside
  the edit mask; no tolerance) and IoU of the top-15% map pixels with the mask, per generator and
  edit type. The masks go through the same evaluation geometry (nearest-neighbour).

Per model and method: mean ± sd over fold models of the per-run mean; method vs random paired
by image (Wilcoxon signed-rank on per-image AUCs pooled over runs, Holm within the experiment).

## GPU-hour estimate

Prediction only. Per fold model about 110 held-out images x 3-4 methods x (map + 42 perturbed
forward passes); integrated gradients adds 32 forward/backward passes per image. Estimated well
under 2 h for the 45 fold models (not timed yet; the kit logs the time of each fold model).
