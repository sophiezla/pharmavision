# E06 · Data efficiency

Written before the experiment runs (PLAN 4.1); the Results section is added afterwards.

## Question

How does each comparison model's performance change when it is trained on a fraction of each
outer training portion?

## Hypothesis

In the development pool, the pretrained models stay near the AUROC ceiling down to small
fractions, while the handcrafted model degrades earlier. On the external block, no directional
hypothesis (ADR-005).

## Models

The five comparison models (ADR-021): `handcrafted-xgb/default`, `convnext-tiny/ft`,
`swin-tiny/ft`, `dinov2-vits14/ft`, EfficientNetV2-S + LoRA r8 (final model, ADR-020), all with
their Phase 2/3 settings and the default recipe, on data v2.

## Arms

| Arm | Description |
|---|---|
| `frac10`, `frac25`, `frac50` | inner training split of each outer fold subsampled to 10 / 25 / 50% |
| 100% | the `p4ref` runs of ADR-021 (no subsampling); not re-run |

## Fixed settings

- Subsampling: grouped by `group_id` and stratified by label, applied to the inner `train`
  split only; `val`, `cal` and the held-out fold are unchanged. At least one group per class is
  kept.
- Draws: 3 per fraction and fold; draw index d ∈ {0, 1, 2} is both the subsampling seed and the
  training seed, so each fraction has 15 runs (5 folds × 3 draws) like the full protocol and
  `seed` in the run ID is the draw index.
- Everything else follows CLAUDE.md §5 and the model configs.

## Metrics

Primary: pooled OOF AUROC (seed-averaged per image, 95% bootstrap CI) per model and fraction.
Secondary: log-loss, ECE, the external block (ADR-005).

## Results

225 runs (5 models × 3 fractions × 15) in the Phase 4 kit (commit d0d2288, RTX 5060), 0
failures; the 100% arm is the 60 `p4ref` runs plus the 15 `e05_efficientnetv2-s_default` runs.
Tables: `results/tables/e06_summary.{csv,md}`, `e06_pairwise.md`. Figure:
`results/figures/e06_learning_curves.{png,pdf}`.

Pooled OOF AUROC (from `e06_summary.csv`):

| model | 10% | 25% | 50% | 100% |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| convnext-tiny/ft | 0.998 [0.995, 1.000] | 0.999 [0.997, 1.000] | 0.999 [0.999, 1.000] | 1.000 [0.999, 1.000] |
| swin-tiny/ft | 0.998 [0.994, 1.000] | 0.999 [0.997, 1.000] | 0.999 [0.998, 1.000] | 1.000 [0.998, 1.000] |
| efficientnetv2-s/default (LoRA r8) | 0.987 [0.979, 0.993] | 0.996 [0.993, 0.999] | 0.999 [0.998, 1.000] | 0.999 [0.998, 1.000] |
| dinov2-vits14/ft | 0.972 [0.959, 0.983] | 0.995 [0.989, 0.999] | 0.996 [0.991, 1.000] | 0.998 [0.993, 1.000] |
| handcrafted-xgb/default | 0.895 [0.867, 0.919] | 0.934 [0.912, 0.954] | 0.968 [0.954, 0.979] | 0.976 [0.964, 0.986] |

## Findings

- The hypothesis holds for the development pool: ConvNeXt-Tiny and Swin-Tiny stay at
  ≥ 0.998 pooled AUROC down to 10%; EfficientNetV2-S and DINOv2 lose some AUROC only at 10%
  (0.987, 0.972). The handcrafted model degrades steadily, from 0.976 at 100% to 0.895 at 10%.
- Calibration suffers at small fractions even where AUROC does not: pooled ECE at 10% is 0.158
  (EfficientNetV2-S), 0.140 (Swin), 0.058 (DINOv2) and 0.034 (ConvNeXt), against 0.004–0.014 at
  100% (`e06_summary.md`).
- External block (descriptive, ADR-005): at 10% and 25%, ConvNeXt, DINOv2 and
  EfficientNetV2-S have lower Mendeley specificity and higher Split E recall than at 100%; for
  ConvNeXt the 25% arm is the extreme (specificity 0.288 / 0.193, recall 0.808 / 0.791). Swin's
  external numbers change little with the fraction. The trade-off moves with the fraction rather
  than improving.
- No test between fractions was pre-registered; the comparisons above are descriptive.
