# E14 · Generative counterfeit synthesis

Written before the experiment runs (PLAN 7.1-7.6); the Results section is added afterwards.
Runs in the combined Phases 5-8 kit (ADR-027).

## Question

Do synthetic counterfeits made from authentic photos help the detector: in-distribution, without
any real counterfeit, and on unseen packaging types (E8)?

## Hypotheses

- A moderate synthetic ratio does not lower pooled OOF AUROC (in-distribution performance is near
  its ceiling, `e06_summary.md`).
- Synthetic-only training is far worse than training with real counterfeits.
- The best ratio arm improves the E8 open-set AUROC of the final model.

## Generators (`synthesis/`, `configs/synthesis/e14.yaml`)

Sources: every authentic development image of data v2 (never the locked test set). Three edits
per source per generator, each seeded by (generator, source, k).

- **OCR** (`ocr.py`): EasyOCR (English), images resized to 1024 px on the long side, boxes with
  confidence >= 0.2, cached per image.
- **procedural** (`procedural.py`): one edit drawn uniformly from those that apply:
  `font_swap, char_substitution, dose_digit_change` (text boxes needed),
  `local_hue_shift (10-30 deg), local_blur_or_rehalftone, logo_warp, local_jpeg (quality 5-20)`
  (region = largest saturated blob outside text, else a random 15-30% box).
- **inpaint** (`inpaint.py`): Stable Diffusion inpainting,
  `stable-diffusion-v1-5/stable-diffusion-inpainting` at revision `8a4288a7` (CreativeML
  OpenRAIL-M; the planned SD 2 repository is no longer public, checked 2026-10-21), fp16, 25
  steps, guidance 7.5, strength 0.75-1.0, one of 5 fixed prompts; mask = one OCR text line
  (+10%) or the region above; only masked pixels are pasted back.

Each synthetic image inherits its source's `group_id`, fold and packaging type (label 1). A run
may train only on synthetic images whose source lies in its own training partition, so val,
cal and held-out images stay real and source-disjoint (Rule 3; `test_leakage.py`).

## Quality check (PLAN 7.4)

A random 100 images per generator (seed 42) come back with the MODELS bundle; Sophie labels
them plausible / implausible on the laptop (`data/annotations/synthetic_quality.csv`). Because
the whole phase runs as one kit (ADR-027), the check is reported next to the results instead of
gating the arms.

## Arms (final-model recipe, ADR-020; full protocol)

| Arm | Training data |
|---|---|
| ratio 0 | the final model's existing runs (`e05_efficientnetv2-s_default`) |
| `<gen>-r05`, `-r1`, `-r2`, `-r4` | real training images + round(ratio x real training counterfeits) synthetic images of one generator (drawn with the run seed) |
| `<gen>-only` | real authentic training images + as many synthetic images as there were real counterfeits; no real counterfeit |

`<gen>` in {procedural, inpaint}: 10 arms, 150 runs.

**Decision (before the run):** the best ratio arm (synthetic-only arms excluded) = highest
pooled OOF AUROC, exact ties -> lower pooled OOF log-loss (ADR-013 rule), recorded in
`results/pipeline/e14_best.json`. It is used for the E8 re-run below, the E15 synthetic-edit
defence and the "both" defence.

**E8 re-run:** the best arm under the E8 protocol (leave one packaging type out, `blister` and
`packaging`, 3 seeds; ADR-021 / E08.md), compared with `e08_efficientnetv2-s_default-no-*`.

## Metrics

Evaluation always on real images only: pooled OOF AUROC with CIs, paired DeLong vs ratio 0
(Holm), external block (ADR-005); E8 re-run: held-out-type AUROC vs the E8 final model.

## GPU-hour estimate

Synthesis: about 370 sources x 3 inpaintings (25 steps each) plus OCR, about 1 h. Training: a
final-model job took 1.09 min (15 `e05` default jobs); runs with synthetic images train on up
to 2.3x as many images: about 150 x 1.09 min x 1.6 = 4.4 h, plus 6 E8 runs.
