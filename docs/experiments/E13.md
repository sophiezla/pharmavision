# E13 · Concept bottleneck and TCAV

Written before the experiment runs (PLAN 6.5-6.8); the Results section is added afterwards.
Runs in the combined Phases 5-8 kit (ADR-027). Needs `data/annotations/concepts.csv` labelled
(PLAN 1.8) before the kit is built.

## Question

Can the counterfeit decision be routed through seven human-readable concepts without losing
accuracy, does correcting the concepts fix mistakes, and does the black-box final model use
these concepts internally?

## Hypotheses

- Concept bottleneck models (CBMs) reach a lower AUROC than the black-box final model
  (300 concept-labelled images of about 550 development images).
- Intervening on uncertain concepts raises task AUROC monotonically in k.
- TCAV finds at least one concept with a score significantly different from random at the last
  stage of the final model.

## Concepts

`text_error, color_mismatch, print_blur, logo_distortion, layout_misalignment,
missing_security_feature, seal_or_package_damage`, each present / absent / unsure on the
300-image stratified development subset (`scripts/select_concept_subset.py`; never the locked
test set). `unsure` and unlabelled values are masked out of the concept loss.

## Arms (`models/concept_bottleneck.py`, `training/cbm.py`; full protocol, 45 runs)

Backbone and recipe of the final model (EfficientNetV2-S + LoRA r8, backbone LR multiplier 1.0,
default recipe, 224 px, ADR-020) -> 7 concept logits -> sigmoid -> linear label head on the
concept probabilities only (Koh et al., 2020).

| Arm | Concept stage | Label head |
|---|---|---|
| `independent` | masked concept BCE, concept-labelled training images | logistic regression on the *annotated* concepts of the labelled training images (unsure / missing imputed with the training prevalence) |
| `sequential` | same | logistic regression on the *predicted* concepts of every training image |
| `joint` | end-to-end: BCE(task) + 1.0 x masked concept BCE on every training image | trained jointly |

Concept stage early stopping: masked concept BCE on the concept-labelled `val` images
(patience 7, at most 30 epochs). Joint: the usual val-AUROC selection (ADR-012). The black-box
reference is the final model's existing runs (`e05_efficientnetv2-s_default`, same folds).

## Intervention (PLAN 6.6)

On held-out images with annotations: replace the k most uncertain predicted concepts
(smallest |p - 0.5| among the annotated ones) with the annotated value (present = 1, absent =
0), k = 0..7; task AUROC per k, pooled over the folds (seed-averaged).

## TCAV (PLAN 6.7; `interpret/tcav.py`)

Final model, the seed-0 fold model of each fold (5). Layers: the last three stages of
EfficientNetV2-S (`blocks.3`, `blocks.4`, `blocks.5`). Positives: up to 50 training-partition
images annotated `present` (a concept with fewer than 10 is skipped and reported). 20 random
negative sets from the training partition; CAV = unit normal of a logistic regression;
TCAV score = fraction of the fold's held-out counterfeits with a positive directional
derivative of the counterfeit logit. Null: 20 CAVs between pairs of random sets. Two-sided Welch
t-test, Holm over concepts x layers within each fold model; a concept counts as used when it is
significant in at least 3 of the 5 fold models (decided before the run).

## Metrics

- `e13_cbm_vs_blackbox`: pooled OOF AUROC (95% CI) of each arm and the final model; paired
  DeLong vs the final model, Holm-corrected; external block as usual (ADR-005).
- `e13_concepts`: per-concept AUROC of the predicted concept on annotated held-out images.
- `e13_intervention` figure: AUROC vs k per arm. `e13_tcav` figure: TCAV score per concept and
  layer with the random distribution.
