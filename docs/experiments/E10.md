# E10 · Calibration, selective prediction, conformal prediction

Written before the experiment runs (PLAN 5.1); the Results section is added afterwards.

## Question

How well calibrated are the comparison models, does temperature scaling help, how much does a
3-seed ensemble improve selective prediction, and which conformal method gives the promised
coverage for both classes?

## Hypotheses

- Temperature scaling does not raise pooled ECE for any model (the deep models are already near
  0.01 pooled ECE in the development pool, `e06_summary.md`), so no large gain is expected.
- The 3-seed ensemble has a lower AURC than a single model.
- Class-conditional (Mondrian) conformal keeps per-class coverage within 2 pp of the target
  where marginal conformal may not.

## Models and data (ADR-026)

The five comparison models (ADR-021): the 60 `p4ref` fold models and the 15
`e05_efficientnetv2-s_default` fold models, no retraining. A prediction-only pass on the GPU PC
(`reliability_predictions.csv` per run) re-predicts each fold model's `val`, `cal` and
held-out images; its held-out probabilities are checked against `oof_predictions.csv`
(largest absolute difference reported). `cal` (12.5% of each outer training portion, fixed per
fold, never used in training) is used only here.

MC dropout (PLAN 5.2) is not run: every model was trained with `head_dropout = 0` (ADR-025).

## Methods

- **Temperature scaling** (`evaluation/calibration.py`): per fold model, one temperature T > 0
  fitted on its `cal` logits by minimizing NLL (LBFGS on log T), applied to its held-out
  logits. The handcrafted model's logit is `log(p / (1 - p))` of its XGBoost probability.
  When every `cal` logit already has the correct sign (perfect separation at 0.5), the NLL keeps
  falling as T -> 0 and has no finite minimizer; then T = 1 (no scaling), and the table reports
  how many fold models had an identifiable temperature. (Added before the kit ran, after the
  self-test and a laptop check showed `cal` sets with no misclassified image, e.g.
  `e05_efficientnetv2-s_default_f0_s0`: AUROC 1.0 on its 55 `cal` images.)
- **Decision rule after calibration:** counterfeit if the calibrated probability >= 0.5 (the
  Bayes rule for 0-1 loss on calibrated probabilities), for the selective and conformal
  analyses.
- **Selective prediction** (`evaluation/selective.py`): confidence = max(p, 1 - p) of the
  calibrated probability. Single model: each fold model's held-out fold. Ensemble: per fold,
  the mean of the three seeds' calibrated probabilities. Risk-coverage curve and AURC (area
  under the selective-risk curve, error = misclassification).
- **Conformal prediction** (`evaluation/conformal.py`): split conformal on `cal`, evaluated on
  the held-out fold, per fold model. Scores: LAC (1 - p_y) and APS (non-randomized: total
  probability of the classes at least as likely as y). Marginal (one quantile) and
  class-conditional (Mondrian, one quantile per true class). Quantile = the
  ceil((n + 1)(1 - alpha))-th smallest score (+inf, i.e. every label, if that rank exceeds n),
  alpha in {0.05, 0.10}. A set of size 2 (or 0) is an abstention.

## Metrics

- Calibration: pooled ECE (15 equal-width bins), Brier and log-loss on the held-out folds
  (each run calibrated with its own T, then seed-averaged per image), before and after
  temperature scaling; paired bootstrap CI of the after - before difference (2,000 resamples).
  Reliability-diagram data per model.
- Selective: AURC, mean ± sd over the 15 fold models (single) and over the 5 folds (ensemble);
  pooled risk-coverage curves for the figure.
- Conformal: per fold model, coverage overall and per class, mean set size, singleton rate,
  abstention rate (size 2), empty-set rate; mean ± sd over the 15 fold models.

## Conformal method choice (PLAN 5.4 acceptance criterion; recorded in an ADR)

For the final model, among {LAC, APS} × {marginal, Mondrian}: keep the methods whose mean
per-class coverage is at least 1 - alpha - 2 pp for both classes and both alpha values; choose
the one with the smallest mean set size at alpha = 0.10; ties go to LAC, then Mondrian. If none
qualifies, choose the one with the smallest largest per-class shortfall, and the ADR records
that the criterion was not met. With about 55 `cal` images per fold (about 25 per class),
coverage varies a lot between fold models; this is reported, not tuned away.

## Findings

- Temperature scaling changes little. The temperature was identifiable (some `cal` image on the
  wrong side of 0.5) for only 3–8 of 15 fold models of each deep model and for all 15 of the
  handcrafted model. No ECE or Brier difference has a CI excluding 0; DINOv2's log-loss rises
  (0.0040 [0.0003, 0.0063]). The first hypothesis (no ECE increase) holds; there is also no gain.
- The 3-seed ensemble has an equal or lower mean AURC than a single fold model for all five
  models (e.g. DINOv2 0.0005 ± 0.0007 vs 0.0018 ± 0.0023; Swin 0.0002 vs 0.0002). This is
  descriptive: no test was pre-registered, and the spread over folds is as large as the means.
- Conformal: LAC Mondrian is chosen for the final model (criterion met, ADR-028). Both APS
  variants miss the 2 pp per-class margin. Marginal LAC also qualifies, so the third hypothesis
  (Mondrian needed for per-class coverage) is not supported for the final model.
- Mean set size is below 1 for every LAC variant of the deep models: because almost every
  held-out image is classified correctly, coverage 1 - alpha is reached through empty sets
  (final model, LAC Mondrian, alpha 0.10: empty-set rate 0.083 ± 0.028, size-2 rate
  0.000 ± 0.000). An abstention here therefore almost always means an empty set, not two
  plausible labels.
