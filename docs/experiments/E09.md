# E09 · Robustness to image corruptions

Written before the experiment runs (PLAN 4.8); the Results section is added afterwards.

## Question

How fast does each comparison model degrade under realistic photo corruptions, and up to which
severity is the final model still usable?

## Hypothesis

AUROC falls with severity for every corruption. Because the development pool's classes differ
in file format and resolution (ADR-005), corruptions that remove resolution or compression cues
(blur, JPEG) are expected to hurt most. No hypothesis about the model ranking.

## Corruptions

`data/corruptions.py`, parameters in `configs/eval/corruptions.yaml`: Gaussian blur, motion
blur, Gaussian noise, JPEG compression, brightness, contrast, rotation, perspective warp,
synthetic glare (additive elliptical highlight). Severities 1–5 per corruption; severity 0 =
identity. Applied after the evaluation geometry (resize, crop), at model-input resolution, with
parameters in model-input pixels. (Amended before any run: the first draft applied them at
native resolution, but in this pool authentic images are about 225 px and counterfeit about
450 px wide (ADR-005), so a fixed blur radius or noise level would hit the two classes
differently.) Random parts (noise, glare position, motion direction) are
seeded per image and corruption from the image ID, so every model sees identical corrupted
images.

## Models and runs

The 15 fold models of each comparison model (ADR-021; `p4ref` runs plus
`e05_efficientnetv2-s_default_*`), no retraining. Each predicts its own held-out fold under
every corruption × severity (9 × 5 = 45 conditions, plus clean).

## Metrics

Primary: pooled OOF AUROC per model × corruption × severity (seed-averaged per image).
Secondary: mean corruption AUROC (mean over the 45 conditions); quality limits.

## Planned tables and figures

- `results/tables/e09_severity.{csv,md}`: AUROC per model × corruption × severity.
- `results/tables/e09_mean_corruption_auroc.md`.
- `results/tables/e09_quality_limits.md`: for the final model, the highest severity per
  corruption at which pooled AUROC ≥ 0.85 (ADR-023; `quality_auroc` in the config); 0 if even
  severity 1 is below.
- `results/figures/e09_severity_curves.{png,pdf}`: one panel per corruption, AUROC vs severity
  per model.

## Done criterion

All 75 fold models evaluated on all 45 conditions; tables and figure built.

## GPU-hour estimate

No training. About 110 held-out images × 46 conditions per fold model, 75 fold models
(≈ 380,000 forward passes at 224 px, plus CPU corruption): about 1 h on the GPU PC.
