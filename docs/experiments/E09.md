# E09 · Robustness to image corruptions

Written before the experiment runs (PLAN 4.8); the Results section is added afterwards.

## Question

How fast does each comparison model degrade under realistic photo corruptions, and up to which
severity is the final model still usable?

## Hypothesis

AUROC falls with severity for every corruption. Because the development pool's classes differ
in file format and resolution (ADR-005), corruptions that remove resolution or compression cues
(blur, JPEG) are expected to hurt most. No hypothesis about the model ranking.

## Corruptions

`data/corruptions.py`, parameters in `configs/eval/corruptions.yaml`: Gaussian blur, motion
blur, Gaussian noise, JPEG compression, brightness, contrast, rotation, perspective warp,
synthetic glare (additive elliptical highlight). Severities 1–5 per corruption; severity 0 =
identity. Applied after the evaluation geometry (resize, crop), at model-input resolution, with
parameters in model-input pixels. (Amended before any run: the first draft applied them at
native resolution, but in this pool authentic images are about 225 px and counterfeit about
450 px wide (ADR-005), so a fixed blur radius or noise level would hit the two classes
differently.) Random parts (noise, glare position, motion direction) are
seeded per image and corruption from the image ID, so every model sees identical corrupted
images.

## Models and runs

The 15 fold models of each comparison model (ADR-021; `p4ref` runs plus
`e05_efficientnetv2-s_default_*`), no retraining. Each predicts its own held-out fold under
every corruption × severity (9 × 5 = 45 conditions, plus clean).

## Metrics

Primary: pooled OOF AUROC per model × corruption × severity (seed-averaged per image).
Secondary: mean corruption AUROC (mean over the 45 conditions); quality limits.

## Planned tables and figures

- `results/tables/e09_severity.{csv,md}`: AUROC per model × corruption × severity.
- `results/tables/e09_mean_corruption_auroc.md`.
- `results/tables/e09_quality_limits.md`: for the final model, the highest severity per
  corruption at which pooled AUROC ≥ 0.85 (ADR-023; `quality_auroc` in the config); 0 if even
  severity 1 is below.
- `results/figures/e09_severity_curves.{png,pdf}`: one panel per corruption, AUROC vs severity
  per model.

## Done criterion

All 75 fold models evaluated on all 45 conditions; tables and figure built.

## GPU-hour estimate

No training. About 110 held-out images × 46 conditions per fold model, 75 fold models
(≈ 380,000 forward passes at 224 px, plus CPU corruption): about 1 h on the GPU PC.

## Results

75 fold models (5 models × 15) evaluated under 45 conditions plus clean in the Phase 4 kit
(commit d0d2288), 0 failures (`results/pipeline/e09_status.json`). Tables:
`results/tables/e09_severity.{csv,md}`, `e09_mean_corruption_auroc.{csv,md}`,
`e09_quality_limits.{csv,md}`. Figure: `results/figures/e09_severity_curves.{png,pdf}`.

| config | mean corruption AUROC | worst condition AUROC | conditions | clean AUROC |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| convnext-tiny/ft | 0.9983 | 0.9831 | 45 | 0.9996 |
| dinov2-vits14/ft | 0.9953 | 0.961 | 45 | 0.9975 |
| efficientnetv2-s/default | 0.9984 | 0.9814 | 45 | 0.9993 |
| handcrafted-xgb/default | 0.9451 | 0.8142 | 45 | 0.9768 |
| swin-tiny/ft | 0.9988 | 0.992 | 45 | 0.9995 |

Quality limits of the final model (`e09_quality_limits.md`): severity 5 for all nine
corruptions, i.e. pooled AUROC ≥ 0.85 at every tested severity.

## Findings

- The four deep models are almost unaffected: mean corruption AUROC 0.995–0.999, and the worst
  single condition is ≥ 0.961. For the final model the lowest is perspective warp at severity 5
  (0.981); DINOv2's lowest is brightness at severity 5 (0.961).
- The handcrafted model is the only one that degrades clearly: Gaussian noise (0.829 at
  severity 5) and perspective warp (0.814 at severity 5) push it below 0.85; rotation brings it
  to about 0.90.
- The hypothesis that blur and JPEG hurt most is not supported: for the deep models these
  corruptions cost ≤ 0.02 AUROC even at severity 5.
- The quality limits are uninformative at the pre-registered 0.85 threshold (ADR-023): the
  final model never falls below it, so the limit is the top tested severity everywhere. The
  severities in `configs/eval/corruptions.yaml` were not strong enough to find a failure
  point. Because corruptions are applied to images whose classes already differ in acquisition
  (ADR-005), the robustness measured here is robustness of that in-pool separation, not
  evidence of robustness on new sources.

### Findings (extension)

- The final model still has no failure point at the 0.85 limit: its lowest condition is
  perspective warp at severity 8 (0.8743 [0.8439, 0.9037]), so its quality limit is 8 for all
  nine corruptions.
- The stronger grid separates the other models. DINOv2 falls below 0.85 under brightness from
  severity 6 (0.705) and reaches 0.432 at severity 8; ConvNeXt falls under brightness at
  severity 8 (0.667). Swin's lowest condition is 0.961 (Gaussian blur, severity 8).
- The handcrafted model's rotation AUROC is not monotonic: 0.8815 at severity 6 (45°) and
  0.9602 at severity 8 (90°).
