# E15 · Adaptive adversary

Written before the experiment runs (PLAN 7.7-7.12); the Results section is added afterwards.
Runs in the combined Phases 6-8 kit (ADR-027).

**Scope change (ADR-029, 2026-10-25, before any run):** the `both` arm (PGD + synthetic edits) is dropped and the arms race runs 3 rounds (45 runs) instead of 5. Defences compared: none, `synth` (E14 best arm), `adv`.

## Question

How easily can a counterfeiter who knows (or can query) the detector make a counterfeit pass as
authentic, and which defence helps at what cost?

## Hypotheses

- Targeted PGD at 4/255 evades the undefended final model on most held-out counterfeits.
- PGD adversarial training lowers PGD success at a small clean-AUROC cost.
- In the arms race, the evasion rate of the score-only attacker falls over the rounds.

## Attacks (`adversarial/`; on each fold model's held-out counterfeits, model-input resolution)

- **Gradient** (`attacks.py`, `torchattacks`): FGSM and PGD-20 (step eps/8, random start),
  L-inf eps in {1, 2, 4, 8}/255, targeted toward authentic.
- **Physical** (`physical.py`, white box): global brightness / contrast / saturation factors in
  [0.8, 1.2] and hue rotation +-0.05 turn (YIQ), Adam 50 steps; a square patch of 5% of the
  image at the top-left corner, Adam 100 steps.
- **Black box** (`blackbox.py`): random search over procedural local edits (hue, blur,
  halftone, JPEG, warp, brightness; random box of 15-40%), 200 score-only queries, stops at the
  first evasion.
- **Success (evasion)**: probability below the fold model's val-selected threshold. Rates are
  reported over the counterfeits that are detected when clean.

## Defences (PLAN 7.10; final-model recipe, full protocol)

| Defence | Runs |
|---|---|
| none | the final model (`e05_efficientnetv2-s_default`) |
| `adv`: PGD adversarial training, eps 4/255, 7 steps of eps/4, half of every batch adversarial | 15 new |
| `synth`: the best E14 ratio arm (E14.md decision) | reused from E14 |
| `both`: PGD adversarial training + the best E14 arm's synthetic images | 15 new |

## Arms race (PLAN 7.11)

5 rounds per (fold, seed) lineage, round 0 = the final model. In round r the score-only
attacker searches evasive edits on every counterfeit of the *training* partition against the
round r - 1 detector; each counterfeit detected when clean and evaded after the edit is saved
(labelled counterfeit, inheriting group and fold). The round-r detector is trained from the
pretrained weights on the training partition plus the evasive images of rounds 1..r. A fresh
search on the held-out counterfeits measures the round's evasion rate. 75 runs.

## Metrics

- `e15_success_vs_eps`: evasion rate vs eps for FGSM and PGD, per defence.
- `e15_defences`: clean pooled OOF AUROC (cost; DeLong vs none) and evasion rates under PGD at
  4/255, colour, patch and black box (gain), mean ± sd over fold models.
- `e15_arms_race`: held-out evasion rate per round (mean ± sd over the 15 lineages), number of
  evasive images added per round, clean AUROC per round.

## GPU-hour estimate

Adversarial training costs about 4.5 forward/backward passes per batch: 15 x 1.09 min x 4.5 =
1.2 h (`adv`), about 2 h with synthetic images (`both`). Arms race: 75 x (1.09 min training +
about 1 min of searches) = 2.6 h. Attack pass: about 1 h for 60 fold models.
